— The method —

Three steps, no black box

  1. I

    Describe the student

    Grades, attendance, study time, home support — 24 signals a school already has on file.

  2. II

    The model weighs them

    An XGBoost classifier, trained on UCI student records, balances every signal against 1,044 real outcomes.

  3. III

    You see exactly why

    Exact TreeSHAP values rank what pushed the score up or down — then a plain-English summary follows.
